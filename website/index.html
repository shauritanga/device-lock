The problem

Credit sales grow revenue — and quietly destroy margin.

Every dealer knows the cycle. The phone leaves the shop, the payments slow down, and recovery costs more than the handset is worth.

Chasing replaces selling

Staff spend their week on phone calls and door visits instead of moving stock. Follow-up is manual, undocumented and easy to skip.

No leverage after the sale

Once the handset walks out, the only pressure you have is goodwill. A customer who stops answering has already won.

Books that don't agree

Schedules in one notebook, payments in another, and no single answer to "how much is actually outstanding this month?"

How it works

Four steps from shop floor to secured loan.

No new hardware, no custom ROM. Any modern Android handset becomes a managed device before the customer leaves the counter.

  1. 01

    Scan to enroll

    Generate a QR from the dashboard and scan it on a factory-reset phone. The device provisions itself as a managed device and enrolls automatically.

  2. 02

    Set the loan

    Enter deposit, term and flat interest. Linda builds the instalment schedule, tracks every payment and flags overdue accounts on its own.

  3. 03

    Remind, then enforce

    SMS reminders go out before the due date. If an account stays overdue, the handset locks to a payment screen — calls to emergency numbers still work.

  4. 04

    Paid means unlocked

    A confirmed ClickPesa payment releases the device within seconds — no staff action, no call to the shop, no argument at the counter.

The platform

Everything the loan book needs, in one place.

Security & trust

You are holding customers' devices. We treat that seriously.

Locking a phone is a powerful action. Every part of the system is built so it can only happen for the right device, from the right shop, for the right reason.

Hardware-backed credentials

Each device's token is sealed in the Android Keystore. Nothing useful sits in plain storage on the handset.

Strict shop isolation

Every record carries its shop identity and is filtered at the database layer, so one dealer can never read another's book.

Roles that match your org

Owner, manager and agent permissions are enforced server-side — an agent cannot release a device just by finding the URL.

Signed payment webhooks

Payment callbacks are checksum-verified before they can mark an instalment paid or unlock a handset. Forged notifications are rejected.

Tamper-resistant agent

Provisioned as a device owner with a verified signing checksum, the agent cannot simply be uninstalled by the customer.

Complete audit trail

Who issued a lock, when it was delivered and when the device acknowledged it — recorded for every command, permanently.

A locked phone is not a dead phone. Emergency calling stays available, and the lock screen shows the customer exactly what is owed and how to pay it — so recovery is a payment, not a confrontation.

Questions dealers ask

Still unsure? Book a demo and we'll walk through your exact setup.

Can the customer remove the lock app?

No. The agent is installed as an Android device owner during provisioning, which blocks uninstalling it and stops a factory reset from returning the phone to an unmanaged state.

What if the phone is switched off or has no internet?

Commands sit in a queue on the server. The moment the handset reconnects it checks in and applies whatever is pending — a lock issued yesterday still lands today. Devices also check in periodically on their own, so nothing depends on the customer cooperating.

Does a locked phone still make emergency calls?

Yes. The lock screen restricts normal use but keeps emergency dialling available, and it clearly shows the outstanding amount plus how to pay — so the customer can resolve it themselves.

How fast does a device unlock after payment?

A verified ClickPesa confirmation triggers the unlock immediately and a push notification wakes the device to collect it — typically under a minute on a connected handset. No staff member has to be at the shop.

Can I run more than one shop on one account?

Yes. Linda is multi-tenant from the ground up. Each shop has its own customers, devices, loans and staff, and owners can see across their branches while agents only see their own.

Which handsets are supported?

Any modern Android phone that supports Android Enterprise provisioning — including the Tecno, Infinix, Samsung and Xiaomi models most commonly sold on credit. Enrollment happens from the factory-reset setup screen via QR code.
